About

I work on natural language processing and large language models at George Mason University, advised by Dr. Marcos Zampieri. My research covers LLM fine-tuning and post-training, text understanding and generation, and building multilingual datasets.

My work has appeared at EMNLP, EACL, AACL-IJCNLP, CIKM, and other venues. I have also shipped NLP and machine learning work in industry at Qualtrics, Cambium Assessment, and Retail Business Services.
